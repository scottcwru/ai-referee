# Journal review-form criteria, combined into constructs

90 criteria from the 16 public forms and guidelines in `step2_journal_forms.md`, plus the eight rows of the Organization Science form (as recorded in Scott's v4 plan), combined into 20 constructs.
Criteria are combined when they name the same construct (for example, statistical methods and testing regression models are one construct; statistical methods and theory are two).
A criterion that names two constructs is split and marked "part". Constructs are ordered by the number of journals that ask about them.

| Construct | Journals | Form criteria combined |
|:--|--:|:--|
| Novelty, originality, or interestingness | 8 | AMJ: Interestingness, innovativeness, novelty; AMD: Surprising discovery; AMD: Not explained by existing theory; AMLE: Originality and importance (originality part); ASQ: Challenge previous understandings; ASQ: Interestingness; AJPS: Creativity/innovation; APA journals: Original; AOM Annals: Value over prior reviews; AOM Annals: New insights; AMP: Rigorous, original analysis (original part) |
| Overall contribution to knowledge | 7 | AMJ: Empirical contribution; ASR: Contribution(s) of research; AMLE: Overall contribution; JIBS: Overall contribution; APA journals: Important contribution; APA journals: Q3: significant contribution; AJPS: Literature engagement and accumulation (accumulation part); AER: Publication-worthiness |
