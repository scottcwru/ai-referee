# Step 2. Journal review forms (retrieved 2026-09-22)

Every criterion below traces to a page retrieved on 2026-09-22; nothing is from model memory. Journals whose forms are not public are listed in Section 3.

Retrieval notes for provenance:
- Several publisher platforms (pubsonline.informs.org, journals.sagepub.com, journals.aom.org, journals.uchicago.edu, apa.org) sit behind Cloudflare/Imperva human-verification walls that block automated retrieval. Where noted, the page was retrieved instead from an Internet Archive (Wayback Machine) snapshot of the same URL; the snapshot is cited. No CAPTCHA was bypassed.
- No journal's structured in-system rating form (ScholarOne / Editorial Manager) is directly retrievable without a reviewer login. Where a journal publishes its form's questions or criteria on a public page, that page is what is recorded.

## 2. Criteria by journal

### Academy of Management Journal (AMJ) — management

- URL retrieved: https://www.aom.org/publications/journals/publishing-with-aom/author-and-reviewer-resources/reviewer-resources/ (retrieved live 2026-09-22)
- Type: public reviewer guidelines ("Outline the Substance of Your Review"); the structured ScholarOne evaluation form itself is not public. (An AMJ editorial describing the form, DOI 10.5465/amj.2009.37308014, is paywalled and was not retrievable.)

| Criterion | Exact wording (brief) | Rigor / contribution / other |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Theoretical contribution | "Does the manuscript test, create, or extend theory?" | contribution |
