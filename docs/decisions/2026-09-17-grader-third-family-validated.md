# The grader: a third model family, validated before use

**Decided:** 2026-09-17 (Scott's v4 plan, Stage 4).

The grader runs on a model family neither reviewer uses. Before it codes any journal review, it is validated against two human coders who are blind to whether an AI or a human wrote each critique; kappa is computed between the grader and each coder and between the coders; the codebook is revised and the cycle repeated until kappa reaches a preregistered threshold. In the study, human coders and a second AI grader also code a random 20 percent of critiques.

**Why:** grader-coder agreement (measurement reliability) and AI-human disagreement (the result) concern different objects, so validating the grader by agreement does not presuppose the result.
