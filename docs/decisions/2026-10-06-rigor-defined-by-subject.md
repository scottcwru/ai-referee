# Rigor is defined by subject; checkability is a separate attribute

**Decided:** 2026-10-06 (Scott; David agreed 2026-10-07). Supersedes the clause of the 2026-09-25 decision that each critique is coded rigor or contribution "by the falsity test."

A critique is rigor if it concerns whether the manuscript's claims are supported by its evidence, design, analysis, or reasoning, and contribution if it concerns the value of the work to the field (importance of the question, novelty, development of theory, positioning in the literature). Whether the asserted defect can be checked is recorded as a separate attribute. Rigor share remains the primary outcome (2026-09-16); checkable rigor is a prespecified secondary outcome.

**Why:** two coders applying the checkability definition as written agreed at kappa 0.09 (`docs/coding-session-2026-10-05.md`). Defining rigor as what can be checked also builds part of the hypothesis into the definition: the theory predicts an AI advantage on rigor because rigor can be verified. Recording checkability separately lets the study test that mechanism instead (`docs/rigor-axis-issue.md`, option A).

**Open:** the definition of the checkability attribute and which critiques carry it.
