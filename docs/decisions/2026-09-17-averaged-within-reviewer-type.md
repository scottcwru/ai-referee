# Rigor share is averaged within reviewer type per manuscript

**Decided:** 2026-09-17 (Scott's v4 plan and September 17 proposal).

For each manuscript, the rigor shares of the two AI reviews are averaged, and those of the human reviews are averaged, separately; the manuscript-level difference is tested with a paired t-test (100 pairs, two-sided alpha .05, power .80, minimum detectable standardized difference 0.28).
