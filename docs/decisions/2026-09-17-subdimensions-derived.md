# Rigor and contribution subdimensions are derived, not asserted

**Decided:** 2026-09-17 (Scott's v4 plan, Stage 3).

Subdimensions come from a corpus of referee reports: a deductive skeleton (Shadish, Cook, and Campbell validity typology for rigor; the form's four contribution rows for contribution), an inductive labeling pass, clustering, and batches until a batch adds no new subdimension. The appendix records the stopping batch. The earlier list of six rigor dimensions is superseded.

**Why:** the six were the authors' choice, not the product of a systematic search. Results by subdimension are reported descriptively, with confidence intervals.

**Open:** how much of this is done by November 1 (plan, Section 4.2).
