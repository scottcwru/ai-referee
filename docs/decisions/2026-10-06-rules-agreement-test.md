# The coding rules are tested on fresh critiques before preregistration

**Decided:** 2026-10-06 (Scott; David agreed 2026-10-07).

Two independent AI coders, blind to the study, code 60 fresh critiques from the public corpus under the revised rules. The rules are frozen for preregistration only when agreement reaches kappa 0.70. A small sample of the coders' disagreements is adjudicated.

**Why:** rules written after seeing the October 5 disagreements cannot be tested on the same critiques.

**Relation to 2026-09-17:** this tests whether the written rules can be applied consistently. It comes before, and does not replace, the validation of the grader against two blind human coders.

**Open:** which agreement measures must reach the threshold, which models code, how the 60 are drawn, and what happens if the threshold is missed. A proposal accompanies the coding instructions.
