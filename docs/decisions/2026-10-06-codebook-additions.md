# The codebook gains qualitative, concept-clarity, and unit-of-analysis entries

**Decided:** 2026-10-06 (Scott; David agreed 2026-10-07). Extends the 2026-09-25 list (`grader/codebook_v8.md`).

The list gains eight entries: the six qualitative gaps found in the BMJ Open pilot (theme construction and interpretation, choice and fidelity of qualitative method, trustworthiness strategies, saturation and sample sufficiency, translation and transcription fidelity, reflexivity), with labels from Guba, Tracy, Gioia and colleagues, and Eisenhardt where they exist; an entry for the clarity of theoretical concepts; and an entry for errors in the unit or level of analysis.

**Why:** the phase 1 corpus is mostly experimental and survey research. Tested on qualitative studies, one qualitative-method critique in three fit only by stretching an entry written for quantitative work (`docs/pilot-bmjopen-qual-2026-10-04.md`); conceptual papers exposed the concept-clarity gap (`docs/pilot-pci-orgstudies-2026-10-04.md`).

**Departures from the counting rule, accepted:** the unit-of-analysis entry is added although its four sightings are one objection recurring across the review history of a single paper (`docs/phase1-review-notes.md`, finding 8). Translation and transcription fidelity has no label in the literature read in Step 1 and carries the authors' label.
