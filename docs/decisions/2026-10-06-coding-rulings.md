# Rulings on the cases that split the coders

**Decided:** 2026-10-06 (Scott; David agreed 2026-10-07).

1. A critique that a procedure was not reported is rigor.
2. A judgment that a design or an analysis is inadequate is rigor.
3. A critique that a citation misreads its source is rigor.
4. Writing and clarity complaints go to a separate category, "other," which is excluded from the rigor share.

**Why:** the October 5 coding session (`docs/coding-session-2026-10-05.md`) traced every rigor/contribution disagreement to two open questions, omissions and methods judgments; the rulings settle both. Writing is about a fifth of critiques (22 percent in the phase 1 corpus) and fits neither rigor nor contribution.

**Open:** which codes make up "other," and rulings on the cases these four do not cover. The coding instructions will carry the rulings with balanced examples.
