# The dimensions are derived from three sources

**Decided:** 2026-09-25 (Scott). Updates the 2026-09-17 decision that subdimensions are derived.

**Superseded in part (2026-10-06):** the clause that each critique is coded rigor or contribution "by the falsity test" is replaced by `2026-10-06-rigor-defined-by-subject.md`; the list is extended by `2026-10-06-codebook-additions.md` and `2026-10-06-literature-entry-split.md`.

The dimensions and subdimensions are derived from three sources: the public review forms of 16 journals plus the Organization Science form, 24 scholarly works on rigor and contribution, and published referee reports (1,852 critiques in 299 reports, in seven shuffled batches). This replaces the earlier skeleton of the Shadish, Cook, and Campbell validity typology plus the form's four contribution rows. Critiques are grouped by the type of error asserted; labels come from the journals first, then the literature, then the authors; a dimension is included if any source supports it; collection stops at the first batch that adds nothing new and repeats no pending item. The result is one list of 11 dimensions with subdimensions (`grader/codebook_v8.md`), and each critique is coded rigor or contribution separately, by the falsity test.

**Why:** the public reports come mostly from journals that review for soundness, not importance, so reports alone under-represent theory and contribution; the forms and the literature supply them. The earlier skeleton was the authors' choice, and the three-source derivation replaces it with a documented procedure.
