# A public summary of AI assistance replaces the session logs in the public repository

**Decided:** 2026-10-21 (David). Changes the contents agreed in `2026-10-20-submission-repository.md`, which Scott approved; Scott to be told.

- The session logs in `docs/ai-log/` stay in the working repository and are not published. They are working records that refer to correspondence and notes not written for publication.
- The public repository has `docs/ai-use.md` instead: the tools and models, what the assistants did by area of work, what we decided and checked, and what failed. It is written from the logs and says which sessions it covers.
