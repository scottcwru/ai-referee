# Complementarity is the headline; substitution is the alternative outcome

**Decided:** 2026-10-06 (Scott; David agreed 2026-10-07).

The proposal presents complementarity between AI and human referees as its headline. Substitution is presented as the alternative outcome the design can also detect.

**Why:** the draft argued both; the design supports either reading (a substitute if the AI finds every rigor error the humans find; complements if each finds errors the other misses), so the proposal must say which is the headline (`docs/phase1-review-notes.md`, finding 7).
