# The rules-agreement test runs after selection, on 150 critiques

**Decided:** 2026-10-09 (Scott). Updates the 2026-10-06 decision that the coding rules are tested on fresh critiques before preregistration.

The test of whether the coding rules (`grader/coding-instructions.md`, `grader/codebook_v9.md`) can be applied consistently is deferred until after the proposal is selected.
It does not run before the November 1 submission.
When it runs, it uses 150 critiques, not 60, drawn in three strata of 50 (quantitative empirical, qualitative empirical, and conceptual or essay work), and the threshold stays at Cohen's kappa of 0.70.
The specifics proposed in `docs/rules-agreement-test.md` (two independent AI coders from families other than the grader's, category and checkability each tested separately, fresh draws on any retest, and adjudication of a sample of disagreements) are the procedure for that test.
This settles the items left open on 2026-10-06.
The test still precedes, and does not replace, the validation of the grader against two blind human coders (decision of 2026-09-17).

**Why:** the test's value is in settling the rules before preregistration, and preregistration follows selection. Running it before the submission would spend effort the Stage 1 deliverables do not require. Three strata of 50 cover the kinds of work that split the coders on October 5.
